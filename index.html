About Licensed

Built by a professional, for professionals.

Licensed started as a CPE tracker built for CPAs — because tracking continuing education credits in spreadsheets is a waste of time for people who spent years earning their credentials.

The insight was simple: every licensed professional faces the same problem. CPAs, attorneys, and physicians all have complex, state-specific compliance requirements, multiple credit categories, and the constant risk of falling short at renewal time. Nobody had built a great tool for any of them.

So we built one. Then three. Licensed is a product of Intuitive Software — a company focused on building simple, focused tools for professionals in niche fields where great software doesn't exist yet.
